Highest View — three directions

All three are front-on, wings open, head looking down — the mark says the name. Drawn as flat vector in a single colour, the way your tattoo is a single flat black. Each is shown large, at nav size, at 16px favicon size, and reversed on light, because a logo is only as good as its worst size.

01

Apex

The heaviest of the three, and closest in feel to the tattoo. Wings built from three stacked planes a side, cut apart by thin gaps so the shape still reads when it shrinks. Solid, grounded, unmistakably a bird of prey.

Vantage

The quiet one. Long soaring wings held almost level, seen from above with the body below them — a bird at altitude, not a bird attacking. Reads as elegant rather than aggressive, and closer to the typography on the site than to the tattoo.

Crest

The one that does two jobs at once. The spread wings form a downward V — the V of View, and the direction of the gaze — with a proper hooded head and beak hanging below the vertex. Only two shapes, so it survives anything, and it is the only one of the three that could eventually stand alone with no wordmark next to it.
